Privacy Policy for NovaPulse Launcher

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This privacy policy explains how NovaPulse Launcher ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how our app operates.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

NovaPulse Launcher does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). All home screen layouts, favorite apps, custom drawer folders, hidden applications, and personal preferences remain strictly local to your device.

2. Device Permissions Used & Why

  • Query All Packages (android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required for the core functionality of a home launcher. Used exclusively on-device to discover, display, and organize your installed applications in the app drawer and home screen grid. This list is never transmitted off your device.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely to load advertisements and obtain user consent through Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

NovaPulse Launcher is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device and usage data for ad serving and analytics purposes:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Coarse location data or general device network connection status
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and diagnostics)

We implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to ensure compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and regional privacy frameworks, allowing users to manage their consent preferences.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps using their services, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Children's Privacy

NovaPulse Launcher is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer support channel on Google Play.
